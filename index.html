OUR STORY

African heritage.
Wakefield heart.

Community begins when people feel they belong.

African United Society in Wakefield is a registered charity promoting social inclusion and public understanding of African culture and heritage.

Our charitable purpose is to support people in Wakefield who face social exclusion because of ethnic origin, religion or belief, particularly members of the African community. Through local connections and engagement with service providers, we help build bridges with the wider community.
